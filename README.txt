FULL-BODY REFERENCE PHOTOS — all 106 species from the v11 species list
======================================================================
Selection method (this set replaces the earlier auto-picked zip):
- ~850 candidate photos were freshly collected from Wikipedia / Wikimedia Commons.
- Every candidate was VISUALLY INSPECTED; for each species the one photo that
  shows the WHOLE fish body (head to tail, side or top view) was selected.
- Rejected: cropped/head-only shots, cooked dishes, jaws/skulls, eggs/embryos,
  maps, diagrams, and illustrations (with one exception below).
- One photo per species, in <species-slug>/01_<species-slug>.jpg (1400px).
- selected-manifest.json records the source page for every photo.

Flagged exceptions (best that exists in free sources):
- sloane-s-viperfish: ILLUSTRATION, labelled as such. No full-body photograph
  exists in free sources (22 candidates checked — all head/body-part close-ups).
- Preserved-specimen photos (whole body, the only kind that exists):
  mariana-snailfish, black-dragonfish, blue-lanternfish, pelican-eel,
  humpback-anglerfish.
- School photos with whole fish clearly visible: yellowfin-tuna, pacific-sardine.
- giant-moray: morays keep their tails in crevices; this is the most-body-visible
  wild shot available.
